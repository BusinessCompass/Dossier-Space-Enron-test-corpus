## Issues for counsel review

### L01 Liquids netting agreement copies

**Proposition and sequence.** January 2002 collation lists precede a 13 February request for ten counterparties and a response reporting no copies yet, a reminder and intended follow-up. The subsequent acknowledgment of that plan is not evidence of delivery. Actors include Susan Bailey, Nony Flores and Tom Moran. Evidence: R001, R002, R036–R038, T052 and T054. [SRC13]

**Assessment and contrary evidence.** The record distinguishes unavailable executed copies, uncertainty over Anadarko's changes/execution, reported execution for Coastal States and Genesis, and partially executed copies for other entities. These distinctions prevent a general inference that all agreements were unexecuted. A repeated Anadarko reference in a Statoil entry is a possible copying error, not an established Statoil fact.

**Gap and disposition.** Final executed-copy availability remains unresolved. The reviewed supplied attachment text does not certify signatures. Counsel question: which exact agreements and execution evidence are material enough to justify targeted Credit or counterparty-record retrieval? Hold as a documentary gap; no concealment finding.

### L02 FPL delivery and binder completeness

**Proposition and sequence.** A January request for electronic copies is followed by a reported binder omission because the document had arrived late, a recipient correction, delivery-failure records and an attachment question answered negatively. Actors include Bailey and the recipients involved in EWS-CCT and prospective onward delivery. Evidence: R004–R012 and R016. [SRC13]

**Assessment and contrary evidence.** Late arrival and routing confusion offer ordinary explanations. Thanks for some emails supports limited receipt, not completeness of the final package. The failure records lack recipient-specific machine-readable detail; the attachment exchange may concern a cover email or separately supplied files.

**Gap and disposition.** No complete transmission package or final onward acknowledgment was established. Counsel question: is actual delivery of a specified document material, and what package or acknowledgment would resolve it? Keep distinct from the Liquids agreement issue; no deliberate-withholding conclusion.

### L06 Termination payment confirmation

**Proposition and sequence.** On 14 February 2002 Bailey asks Moran and Gonzalez whether listed termination payments were received; the request is forwarded to Apollo, who acknowledges the information need that day. On 4 March Bailey reports no response to Shackleton. The request includes $21,521,740 against BP Capital Energy Fund, L.P.; this is an unverified listed amount, not a proved debt or loss. Evidence: P002, P006, P016, P001 and S0796. [SRC13–SRC16]

**Assessment and contrary evidence.** The acknowledgment contradicts a literal claim that nobody replied, while leaving the substantive payment question unanswered. Reconciliation workload, receipts awaiting matching and answers through other channels remain possible. The Shackleton copy is not independent confirmation of nonpayment. Similarly named BP entities and other amounts must remain separate.

**Gap and disposition.** No linked bank receipt, cash ledger, offset or substantive reconciliation was identified in the selected searches. Several candidate custodians lacked relevant-period correspondence. Counsel question: what exact obligation, receipt or reconciliation evidence is required before payment status can be assessed? Hold pending that source. Do not aggregate requested amounts as losses.

### L04 Bazor and Destin volumes

**Proposition and sequence.** On 8 December 2000 Pereira reports November receipt allocation of 7,591 MMBtu against 31,112 scheduled per Rebel's directions and anticipates higher-priced cashout with Destin. The numerical difference is 23,521 MMBtu. Later messages correct transport/fuel deductions and report cashout losses on the production, without a sum or explicit settlement linkage to that difference. Actors include Pereira, Stevens Hollister, operational contacts identified as Joe Casa/Casas and Jane Saladino, and contract preparer Stacy Dickson; identity variants are not independently resolved. Evidence: G0040–G0048, D0001, DP001 and E0003. [SRC17–SRC19]

**Assessment and contrary evidence.** Earlier allocation delays concern October and cannot simply explain November. A later BTU dispute has not been linked to the November difference. Fuel at 0.3% of 7,591 is about 23 MMBtu and does not explain the gap; an earlier literal 0.003% statement remains an unresolved inconsistency. Open corrections support an operational explanation without verifying the final accounts.

The confirmation attachment identifies Bazor/Masonite and Destin meter 996800. Its final system version contains an underproduction exception addressing deficiency-default and cashout responsibility. The 9 March 2001 covering message says Dickson had not seen executed documents and would look for signed copies. This is material contract context, with applicability unverified. Blank extracted signature fields and unavailable signed copies do not establish no agreement. The linked master text includes formation and confirmation procedures; no legal enforceability determination has been undertaken.

**Gap and disposition.** No November final allocation, nomination revision or cashout settlement was located in this bounded exercise. Ring's narrow meter/plant search returned no matches; Keiser's archive lacked demonstrated relevant-period coverage. Counsel question: which applicable transaction terms, final meter allocation and ENA/Destin settlement would distinguish production shortfall, measurement/reconciliation effects and the parties' allocation of responsibility? Hold pending those records. The reported difference is neither a quantified monetary loss nor evidence of intent.

## What the pilot demonstrated and what it did not

The useful result is practical and bounded: native sources and their production representations can be traced, methods amended before expanded work, corrections and duplicates retained, and propositions narrowed as evidence develops. The Bazor contract exception and Apollo acknowledgment show why contrary evidence must travel with the initial concern.

Method additions were useful for orientation and experimentation, but this record does not justify making chunking, concept mapping or network visualization mandatory. S25 supplies no demonstrated retrieval-coverage benefit in its controls. None of the small experiments establishes general superiority, cost savings or unattended reliability. File-access failures, parser behavior and unavailable visual QA remain operational limitations. Tool success and hashes cannot substitute for semantic review.

The appropriate next phase, if authorised, would begin with a concrete evidential question and suitable source coverage. It should not treat unresolved leads as accumulated suspicion or repeat broad searches without a reason to expect useful evidence.
