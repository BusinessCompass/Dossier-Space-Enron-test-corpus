# ISO/IEC 27037:2012 application profile - Session 16

## Status and scope

ISO/IEC 27037:2012 is applied as best-efforts guidance for identification, collection, acquisition, and preservation of potential digital evidence. The official ISO catalogue describes it as published and shows revision activity as of the session check on 2026-08-29.

Official reference: https://www.iso.org/standard/44381.html

This session does **not** claim ISO certification, formal conformity, or clause-by-clause compliance. The full licensed standard text has not been supplied to the session. The assessment therefore uses the official published scope and recognised evidence-handling controls, while making limitations and conflicts explicit.

## Operational application

The session will:

- identify the corpus, storage location, technical context, provenance information, responsible roles, and uncertainties;
- distinguish the received source copy from working, derived, evidential, and reporting artefacts;
- document that collection/acquisition and copying occurred before this formal session and avoid reconstructing missing history as fact;
- preserve native structure and metadata, prohibit writes within the corpus, and evaluate effective write protection;
- create deterministic inventory and integrity records using recorded tools, parameters, times, exceptions, and hashes;
- keep material handling actions attributable, chronological, reproducible, and reviewable;
- record every transfer, access, transformation, exception, and custody gap that becomes known;
- verify preserved artefacts can be reopened and reconciled;
- require explicit assurance before analytical activation.

## Conflict handling

Potential alignment issues are recorded in `01_ACTUAL/ISO_IEC_27037_CONFORMANCE_CONFLICT_REGISTER.csv` using these states:

- `satisfied`
- `partial`
- `conflict`
- `not_applicable`
- `unable_to_determine`
- `open_not_tested`

An issue is never silently upgraded to satisfied. Remediation, limitations, and residual risk must be carried into OBSERVED, ROMER, and assurance.
