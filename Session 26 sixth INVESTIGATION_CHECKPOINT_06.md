# S26 checkpoint 6 — contract context changes assessment of L04

## Finding

The final system version of the Bazor/Masonite confirmation contains an express underproduction exception addressing deficiency default and imbalance/cashout responsibility. This is material contrary evidence to an assumption that the scheduled/allocated difference itself demonstrates a contractual failure by EOTT. Applicability and actual settlement are not established. No legal enforceability finding is made.

D0001/D0002 are byte-identical native attachments (SHA256 28cb00ed82317833274f50d064f3a48806ad0b8292accbe5f5bfda3a2dfe2c2b), not two independent confirmations. XML Attachment relationships link them to DP001/DP002, copies of Dickson's 9 March 2001 email to Pereira. Dickson says she has returned from four months' maternity leave, has not seen executed documents, supplies the final versions on the system, and will seek signed copies if EOTT returned them during her absence. This proves a reported copy/status gap, not nonexecution. The confirmation text bears 1 October 2000; the March attachment timestamp is not proof it was created or agreed in October.

## Document terms observed

The confirmation names EOTT Energy Operating Limited Partnership and Enron North America Corp.; covers available volumes from Bazor and Masonite wells after processing, shrinkage and fuel; gives an estimate of 6,000–8,000 MMBtu per day; and identifies Destin meter 996800. The estimate is not a verified nomination or monthly production volume. Pricing specifies October Gas Daily and November 2000–February 2001 average of three Inside FERC indices plus $0.01 less transport/fuel, consistent with the earlier correspondence.

The Other Provisions paragraph states that Customer shall not be liable for Seller's Deficiency Default under section 3.2 or corrections of imbalances, cashouts and penalties under section 7.2 where the well does not produce quantities sufficient to equal DCQ. It also excludes a quoted scheduled-versus-actual imbalance sentence. These are textual observations; determining what took effect requires the agreement/transaction formation evidence and applicable final versions. Do not equate bilateral EOTT/ENA terms with the separate ENA/Destin pipeline settlement.

The sibling attachment E0003 (duplicate E0006), native SHA256 ea66499abc564832c490fd523d9d97d11413a1dbefb32e1720c5100ca67623f1, is supplied text of an ENFOLIO master agreement identifying the same parties and an effective date of 1 August 2000. Explicit XML relationships connect both confirmation and master to each parent. Selected master sections 2.1–2.5, 3.2 and 7.2 were inspected, not every provision. The text includes transaction-formation and confirmation procedures beyond a returned signature; blank signature fields in extracted text therefore cannot establish no agreement. Native binaries were hashed, not executed or visually rendered; extracted text cannot certify absence of signatures or other graphical content. Formatting/character artifacts remain in supplied text.

## Search and limitations

Amendment 8 first inventoried available custodian archive names: no separately named Casa/Casas or Saladino ZIP was present. Their correspondence may exist in other archives. Dickson was selected because the earlier contract correspondence names her explicitly. Her 518 XML-linked messages include 258 recorded dates in August 2000–April 2001, 256 correspondence candidates. Crucially the year distribution contains no 2000 dates: this is 2001 follow-up coverage, not demonstrated contemporary November coverage. Seventy-one dates are in 1980 and one in 1316; implausible dates were not treated as historical coverage. Content searches did not hard-exclude dates.

Initial Dickson search: 930 documents, two attachment hits and no message hits. Expanded EOTT/meter search: six hits (two parent copies, two confirmation copies, two master copies). Pereira expanded operational search: 873 documents, 108 hits (99 messages, nine non-message texts). EOTT/meter follow-up: 73 messages. Results overlap earlier hits and folder copies; counts are not independent events. Selected linked correspondence was reviewed; not every broad operational hit was adjudicated. No linked November nomination, final allocation statement or cashout invoice/ledger was identified in this pass; that is not corpus-wide absence.

K0011 asks whether Molly received the contract by email in September, not whether she signed it. K0030 gives an invoice fax route in November, not invoice contents or settlement proof. K0006 reports another system-price correction in November 2001; it must not be misdated as November 2000 resolution. Supplied operational attachments from the broad Pereira search concern other pipeline names/dates and have not been substantively audited as spreadsheets.

Both source ZIPs matched the S16 baseline before/after and remain in place. A parent-trace attempt failed before opening the archive because Python used cp1252 for a UTF-8 JSON file; explicit UTF-8 fixed it, and the failure/retry is recorded. The coverage and follow-up scripts inherited inaccurate script-path strings in initial action entries; checkpoint 6 records the actual executed filenames: bazor_coverage.py, bazor_search.py, bazor_parents.py and bazor_followup.py. Full result sets and relationships are preserved under 02_RESULTS/bazor-*.
