These demonstrate earlier allocation delays, not a proven cause of the November difference. On 17 November she supplies October daily allocations/prices (G0041).
- 21 November: G0043 literally states fuel of 0.003%; December correspondence states 0.3%. Preserve the inconsistency: a possible percent/decimal error is an inference, not a source correction.

The arithmetic difference is 23,521 MMBtu. It is not a loss figure, validated final imbalance or evidence of intent. A separate clarification gives $0.24 transport plus $0.0022 ACA per MMBtu, 0.3% fuel, and distinguishes gross-volume billing from net-volume billing (G0046). This is a contemporaneous reported loss, stronger than the earlier forecast, but supplies no amount, settlement identifier or explicit allocation of the loss to November's difference. ## Assessment and next evidence

An operational explanation involving startup, nominations, allocation timing, measurement or billing treatment remains plausible. The record also leaves a substantial reported scheduled/allocated difference and unquantified reported cashout losses. Open corrections and correspondence weigh against an unsupported inference of concealment, without proving all accounting was correct.

This calculation tests scale and internal consistency only; it does not validate the contract or allocation basis.
